## 4. App Review notes

```
ONE Facilities Management is an employee-only app for staff of ONE FM. There is no sign-up: accounts are created by the employer, so the app cannot be used by the public. The listing is public so employees can install it.

HOW TO LOG IN
Use the review account entered in the App Store Connect "Sign-in required" fields.
Employee ID: see App Store Connect
Password: see App Store Connect

HOW CHECK-IN WORKS
After login, open check-in from the home screen. The app shows a map with your position. Allow location when asked, then confirm check-in. The app checks that you are at the assigned site and shows a success screen. Check-out works the same way.
Check-in is open daily between 09:15 and 00:59 UTC (12:15 to 03:59 Kuwait time). Outside this window check-in is not available. Please test inside it.
Face check is turned off for the review account, so no face video is recorded during review.

WHY CAMERA AND LOCATION ARE NEEDED (Guidelines 2.1, 4.2, 5.1.1)
- Location (while in use only): confirms the employee is at the assigned work site when checking in. The app does not track location in the background.
- Camera: records a short face video to verify the employee's identity at check-in and check-out. It is also used for profile and document photos. This is off for the review account.
- The purpose strings in the app explain each request.

DATA DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Accounts are created by the employer, so deletion is requested through HR or info@one-fm.com. Check-in records are kept for audit and employment-record purposes.

The app has an English and an Arabic interface and runs on iPhone only.
```
